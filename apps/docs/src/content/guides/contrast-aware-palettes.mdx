## Ramp in lightness, map by chroma

Contrast is set by luminance, and in OKLCH luminance closely tracks
lightness. A ramp that varies only `l` at a fixed hue and chroma therefore
orders its steps by contrast. [`lightnessScale`](/api/core/lightnessScale) builds one,
copying chroma, hue and alpha from the base color.

The extremes of such a ramp leave the gamut: a near-white blue cannot keep
chroma `0.16`. Map every step with [`toSrgbGamut`](/api/core/toSrgbGamut).
Its default method lowers chroma only, so each step keeps the lightness, and
roughly the contrast, the ramp gave it. The `'css'` method would move
lightness and break that ordering (see
[Requested vs displayed](/concepts/requested-vs-displayed)).

```ts title="ramp.ts"
import { contrastRatio, lightnessScale, parse, toSrgbGamut } from 'color-kit';

const white = parse('#fff');
const ramp = lightnessScale(parse('oklch(0.6 0.15 250)'), 5, [0.2, 0.9]).map(
  (step) => toSrgbGamut(step),
);
ramp.map((step) => contrastRatio(step, white).toFixed(2));
// → ['18.11', '10.23', '4.85', '2.43', '1.34']
```

## Read pass/fail off the plane

Checking each step with [`contrastRatio`](/api/core/contrastRatio) tells you
which steps pass. A contrast region tells you why, and where the edge is
for every chroma at once: the plane's `contrastRegion` query returns the
contour of the area that meets a level against a reference.
Against white, everything darker than the contour passes; against black,
everything lighter. A step inside a region passes the matching check exactly,
because the region solver and `contrastRatio` measure the same displayed
luminance (see [Contrast](/concepts/contrast#regions-agree-with-checks)).

Some consequences you can see in the example below:

- **Text on both.** AA against white needs relative luminance at most
  0.1833; AA against black needs at least 0.175. The two edges nearly touch,
  and most ramps have no step between them.
- **Mapped steps sit on the gamut edge.** Where the ramp's chroma does not
  fit, the step lands on the sRGB boundary at its own lightness instead of at
  the ramp's chroma.
- **APCA.** Pass `metric: 'apca'` with an `apcaPreset` (`'body'`,
  `'large-text'` or `'ui'`) to draw APCA regions instead; `apcaPolarity` and
  `apcaRole` choose the direction.

In React, [`ContrastRegionLayer`](/api/react/ContrastRegionLayer) draws the
same region over a `ColorArea`, so a picker can show the passing area live.

## Edge cases

- **Give it a size.** The area renders a plain `div` with no dimensions of
  its own; children such as `ColorPlane` fill it. A zero-height area renders
  nothing to drag.
- **Axes must differ.** Both axes on the same channel throw outside
  production builds. Production builds warn once and swap the y axis to
  another channel instead. Y values increase upward.
- **One thumb.** A `<Thumb>` child (found through nested elements) or the
  `thumb` prop replaces the default thumb; the prop wins when both are
  present. More than one throws outside production builds and is reduced to
  the first with a warning in production. Use the `thumb` prop when the
  thumb is wrapped in your own component, memoized, or comes from another
  module, since the area cannot see a `<Thumb>` inside another component's
  render. `showDefaultThumb={false}` renders none.
- **Provider or props.** Without a `<Color>` ancestor the area needs both
  `requested` and `onChangeRequested`; with neither it throws. The props win
  over a provider when both are present.
- **Disabling mid-drag.** Setting `disabled` while a drag is in progress
  cancels it and drops the pending update, so the value cannot change after
  the area is disabled.
- **Clicks focus the thumb.** Pointer down moves focus to the thumb, so
  arrow keys continue from where the pointer left off.
